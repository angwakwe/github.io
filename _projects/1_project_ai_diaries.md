The rising role of AI in various industries requires new skills in the job market. Teaching staff at the University of Surrey wanted to understand the skills employers expected from candidates. This information would inform redesigning curriculum and delivering lectures on topics that prepared students for their professional careers. 

## The Task

Identify the AI-related skills employers expect from job candidates to inform university curriculum development.

## The Problem

The project required a systematic approach to analyzing more than 120,000 job descriptions collected from Reed, a UK job board, to identify employer demand for AI-related skills.

## Text Mining and AI-Assisted Coding

I developed a workflow combining text-mining techniques with AI-assisted coding to examine 3,898 job descriptions for references to AI-related skills.

My approach also involved manually reviewing documents to assess accuracy and refining the code to address inconsistencies in how relevant information was collected.

## The Outcome

From May 2026 - August 2026 I analyzed 3,898 job descriptions and translated the findings into a formal report that shared with faculty members throughout the department.

The findings will also inform a teaching seminar that I am scheduled to co-deliver with a colleague in November 2026.

The project demonstrates how computational methods can support large-scale document analysis while retaining human oversight to interpret and validate of findings.
